What this prototype does

This is manual practice with acceleration, braking, and five sensors that detect walls and obstacles. Steering widens at high speed; you cannot turn in place. Speed is measured in simulation units, not km/h.

Both steering directions together mean straight. No accelerator means brake; ↓/S also brakes and takes priority over accelerate. There is no reverse gear.

Sensor values are normalized from 0 (at a surface) to 1 (nothing within 300 units). CSV recordings include five normalized distances and speed, plus steering and throttle labels. This page supports fixed and seeded random practice layouts. Load one trained model here for practice, or multiple models on the race page.
