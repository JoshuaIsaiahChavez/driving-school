How this race will work

Every car gets the same course, starting position, speed, sensors, and physics. Ghost cars can overlap; they detect only walls and obstacles, not other cars. Driver numbers and labels identify overlapping cars.

A collision ends only that car's run. The others continue until they finish, collide, or reach 120 simulated seconds. The camera follows the leading active car; the overview shows every car. Losing page focus pauses the race.
